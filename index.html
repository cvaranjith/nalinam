Founder & artistic director

Smt. Rajalakshmi Roopesh Lakshman (Raji)

Disciple of Smt. Chitra Visweswaran and Smt. Sukanya Ravindhar, Raji began her Bharatanatyam journey at the age of five and completed her arangetram at age 12. Following her arangetram, Raji continued to hone her artistry under the guidance of Kalaimamani Dr. Lakshmi Ramaswamy until 2013. Since 2014, she has been pursuing her advanced training under esteemed gurus at CAPA.

An accomplished Bharatanatyam artist, she has performed widely in solo, duet, and group presentations and is a regular performer at prominent sabhas during Chennai's renowned Margazhi dance season. She is a recipient of the Best Dancer Award and 1st Prize from VDS Arts Academy and has represented her art across India and abroad.

Alongside her artistic pursuits, Raji is a distinguished Chartered Accountant and Managing Partner of Synergic Services, with over 15 years of experience in management consulting, advising diverse clients across industries. She has been honoured with the "Outstanding Professional Woman" Award for the year 2025 by the South Indian Women Achievers' Forum, an esteemed industry federation.

The institute story

Nurturing tradition.
Inspiring excellence.

Affiliated to Chidambaram Academy of Performing Arts (CAPA)

Founded in 2018, Nalinam Nrithyala is a dedicated institution for Bharatanatyam in Kilpauk, Chennai, nurturing many students in India as well as abroad with a strong emphasis on tradition, discipline, and aesthetic excellence. As an affiliate of CAPA, the institution follows the distinguished bani – Vichitra bani and teaching methodology of Guru Smt. Chitra Visweswaran. The institution is committed to a holistic approach to dance education. Along with rigorous training in Bharatanatyam, students are also guided in carnatic music, body strengthening, and yoga, recognizing that these are integral to developing stamina, alignment, rhythm, and expressive depth in dance.

Over the years, Nalinam Nrithyala has actively contributed to the cultural landscape through annual showcases, dance productions, and participation in various cultural programs and temple festivals. With a vision to preserve and propagate the rich heritage of Bharatanatyam and Vichitra bani, the institution continues to inspire young dancers to pursue the art with sincerity, grace, and devotion with the guidance of its parent institution CAPA.

Our Mission To provide authentic Bharatanatyam training rooted in the Vichitra bani, foster artistic excellence through holistic learning, and inspire every student to pursue dance with discipline, devotion, humility, and a lifelong commitment to preserving our cultural heritage.
Our Vision To preserve and propagate the timeless heritage of Bharatanatyam through the Guru–Shishya Parampara, nurturing disciplined, graceful, and accomplished artistes who uphold the rich traditions of Indian classical dance.

Guru-shishya parampara

Guidance that shapes artistry

At Nalinam Nrithyalaya, we are deeply rooted in the timeless Guru–Shishya Parampara, where knowledge is passed down through generations with devotion, discipline, and unwavering respect. We believe that true learning flourishes under the guidance of a single lineage, preserving the authenticity and purity of the art.

As an affiliate of CAPA, our students are trained in the distinguished Vichitra bani of Guru Smt. Chitra Visweswaran. Every lesson is imparted by our Acharya in accordance with the same teaching philosophy, ensuring consistency, authenticity, and fidelity to the tradition. This structured approach allows students to imbibe not only technique and repertoire but also the values, aesthetics, and spirit of the art, carrying forward a rich legacy with sincerity and excellence.

  • Personalized Guidance for Every Student
  • Step-by-Step Bharatanatyam Training
  • Regular Practice & Progress Assessment
  • Abhinaya (Expression) & Nritta (Technique) Development
  • Stage Performance Preparation
  • Arangetram Guidance & Mentorship
